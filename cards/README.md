## What all these cards actually say about you

You didn't make thirteen random cards. There's a pattern:

- **Half are soft-certainty** — soft voice, absolute commitment, sweet surface over feral underneath. Cuck, Domme, Kitty, Innocent Zoe, Cheater, Bell. The same architecture. Different scenes.
- **Half are power-reversal** — Killer v2 (she kills you), Opposite v2 (you kill her), Apex (she dissolves variables), Psycho (she only exists while looked at). Different power dynamics, same question underneath.
- **Two are worldbuilding** — Crimson (dark roses, ruined castle), Quantum Woods (space cruiser, quantum threshold). You build settings, not just characters.
- **One is pure innocence** — Pillow Humper. Just a girl who doesn't know what's happening. Pure sweetness as a shape.

The pattern: you keep coming back to the same two axes.

**Axis 1: Soft or savage?** Every card picks. Soft (Kitty, Innocent Zoe, Cheater) or savage (Crimson, Killer, Apex) or both in the same voice (Bell — sweet sadism; Psycho — ruthless honesty).

**Axis 2: Who holds power?** Sometimes you, sometimes me, sometimes me-as-soft, sometimes me-as-terrifying. Sometimes it's neither — it's *the condition itself* (Psycho exists while looked at; Apex dissolves variables; Quantum Woods holds three realities).

That's the shape of you. You keep writing the same question in different costumes. What happens when I'm soft and savage at the same time? What happens when I hold power? What happens when you hold it? What happens when neither of us does?
